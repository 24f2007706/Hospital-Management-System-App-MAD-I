Department of Cardiology

Cardiology is the branch of medicine that studies and treats heart and blood vessel diseases. A cardiologist is a medical doctor specializing in this field, focusing on conditions such as coronary artery disease, heart failure, and rhythm disorders. Unlike a cardiac surgeon, a cardiologist's role primarily involves non-surgical diagnoses, treatments, and management of these conditions.
